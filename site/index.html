How it works

A 1.5 kW induction coil. A current of warm water. Repeat.

Cold water enters the rear convective intake, passes around a ferrite-cored induction coil heating it directly through electromagnetic flux, and exits as a slow vortex at the nose. Buoyancy carries the warm layer outward in a stable ring.

STEP 01

Cold water in

Convective intake at the rear pulls ambient water through a fine-mesh prefilter.

STEP 02

Induction heat

A 1.5 kW ferrite-cored coil transfers heat directly to the water — 96% efficient, no exposed elements.

STEP 03

Vortex outlet

A spiraled nose manifold ejects heated water in a wide, slow vortex that won't sting or scald.

STEP 04

Closed-loop sensing

Eight IR + thermocouple sensors map the surrounding zone and modulate output to hold your set temp.
